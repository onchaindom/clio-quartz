---
title: Concepts
description: The named ideas, formal devices, and theoretical moves that organize how the scene reads itself.
---

A *concept* page names an idea the scene uses to think with — a formal device (density-as-value, post-authorship), a critical frame imported from theory (the index, network spirituality), or a self-named move (traitmaxxing). Concept pages exist when a term recurs across multiple sources or works, and especially when insiders use the term themselves.

The aim is to give a careful reader the vocabulary to follow the scene's internal arguments — the words it uses to describe what it does, in the order it learned them.
