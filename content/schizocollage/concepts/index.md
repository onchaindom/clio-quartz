The pages collected here are the named ideas the schizocollage scene has produced for itself, plus the critical frames that have proved load-bearing in reading it. Some names come from inside the scene — [[network-spirituality]] from Charlotte Fang, [[traitmaxxing]] from Monk Antony's September 2025 thread — and some come from the criticism written about it: Brian Droitcour's [[visible-seams-aesthetic]] in *Seams and Synthesis*, Bauman's [[png-layer-compositing]] in *Drifella III: Room for Complexity*. The press includes both kinds of name. A scene's vocabulary is partly what its practitioners declare and partly what its readers find load-bearing; the wiki records what is actually in the critical record either way.

These concept pages exist as standalone entries rather than as paragraphs inside [[formal-grammar]] because each one carries argumentative weight beyond a single rule. They get a page when the same idea appears across multiple figures, multiple critical sources, or multiple works. Concepts that have not yet earned that weight stay as enumerated rules in the formal-grammar file.
