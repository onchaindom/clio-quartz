*Little Swag World #1219* (2023) is a sculpture from [[supermetal-bosch|Supermetal Bosch]]'s *Little Swag World* project — the inverse of [[mons-estalibur|*Mons*]] in method, beginning with a fashion mood-board collage and asking the AI model to smooth the references into a single coherent figure.

## Close looking

A bright lime-green plush frog-knight stands centred on a clean off-white seamless [[ground]], [[photograph|photographed]] in the same product-shot register as [[mons-estalibur|*Mons "estalibur"*]]. The body is a chunky, wide-stanced creature with a frog's smooth head and rounded throat, two large round eyes with diamond pupils, a closed half-smile, and a soft pink interior visible just below the lip — heavy [[mass]] for its scale, anchored at the centre of the field. Knit armour wraps the chest and shoulders in heavy lime-green cable, [[visible-seams-aesthetic|seam-lines visible]] but tightly finished, the [[texture]] of yarn doing as much identifying work as the [[silhouette]]; a metal-studded belt with a small sword hilt cinches the waist. Tall, mirror-polished metal spikes — four of them — rise from the back of the head and shoulders at sharp angles, catching highlights along their tapered edges. In the right hand the creature holds a fully detailed silver scimitar with a baroque cast hilt; the left hand grips a heater shield embossed with a heraldic crest in pewter and gold. The feet are checked black-and-white plush in a [[png-layer-compositing|pixel-pattern]] cuff. The [[palette]] holds at one wet lime-green chord, with the polished metal spikes and weapons supplying all the cool relief. Reads first: the wet-green body and the four polished spikes, the figure's [[silhouette]], easy and complete. Reads second: the metal weapons, which the AI-smoothing has resolved into the body's logic instead of leaving them as [[traitmaxxing|stacked traits]]. Reads last: the checked feet and seam stitching, the small reminders that this is hand-fabricated to look as if it were not.
