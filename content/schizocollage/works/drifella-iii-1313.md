*Drifella III #1313* (2025) is a single room from [[evil-biscuit|Evil Biscuit]]'s [[drifella-iii|Drifella III]] whose central panel is, unusually, a small framed cameo at the centre of two large square canvases.

## Close looking

The room reads as a wide cream wall with three pictures of unequal size hung in a row; the floor below holds two scattered toy figures and a fishing reel pushed flush against the right edge. Left panel: a tall square of pale wash dominated by an inverted, beige-and-grey skin rendered in scribble and pencil — limbs collapsed, the figure mid-fold — with a small dark animal head with bared teeth bleeding ink down the centre, and a knot of cool-blue scribble in the upper third where colour pools without resolving into a face. Centre: a small, ornate gold baroque frame, screwed to the wall, holding a tiny portrait of a [[mifella|Crucifella]]-style character in a black peaked cap, the painting itself the size of a postcard inside the heavy gilt. Right panel: a near-black square of midnight purple speckled with little starbursts and diamonds, the [[evil-biscuit|Drifella]] silhouette gauzily visible inside it, a [[png-layer-compositing|pixel-square mouth]] stamped where the face should be. The size mismatch is the work — the central cameo is the punchline against the two big canvases beside it. Reads first: the gold frame, drawn to it by gravity. Reads last: the fishing reel on the floor, an indoor still-life note that returns the [[room-scale-attention|room]] to its set-design.
