*Drilady #598* (2024) is one work from [[parker-ito|Parker Ito]]'s [[drilady|Drilady]] collection, the @dril quotation pulled to the surface as the picture's primary content.

## Close looking

The panel is keyed to a single deep brick-red ground that fills the whole field. Three large pink orchid blossoms — photographically rendered, with yellow-flecked centres — sit stacked vertically along the lower right, the topmost flower opening toward the centre and a thin pink tongue extending from its throat. Behind and partly under the orchids, a Drilady doll torso shows in fragments: a flat tan shoulder, a strap, a [[png-layer-compositing|stamped]] basketball-style pendant printed *MENSA* across the chest. Across the upper two-thirds of the panel, in stacked all-caps lettering with a chrome bevel and metallic gradient, the lines: *I HAVE NO / MONEY / AND ALSO / I'M GAY*. The font is the YouTube-thumbnail / pop-rap album-cover register — silver edge, sharp drop shadow. At the lower edge, a flat red bar stamped *FREE* in white serif terminates the picture, the same caption tag used at the bottom of [[the-constant-fella-666|The Constant Fella №666]]. Reads first: the chrome text, which is the picture's content as much as the figure is. Reads second: the orchids, the only photographic register, soft against the rest of the flat colour. Reads last: the *MENSA* badge, a [[traitmaxxing|trait-detail]] tucked behind the flowers — the [[mifella|Milady-fork]]'s longstanding habit of letting captions tell.
