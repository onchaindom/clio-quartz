# Lineage

## Ancestors (pre-2021)

| Period | Node | Transfer |
|--------|------|----------|
| c. 1860–1880s | Victorian photocollage (aristocratic British women) | Layered + irreverent collage, domestic space documented through pop-culture fragments. Monk Antony traces this to the [[concepts/traitmaxxing|traitmaxxing]] lineage in a 2025 thread. |
| 1956 | Richard Hamilton, *Just what is it that makes today's homes so different, so appealing?* | The proposition that all art is equal, high and low placed in shared rooms. Bauman invokes this directly for [[entities/drifella-iii|Drifella III]]'s room-scale ambitions. |
| 1962 | Andy Warhol, *Marilyn Diptych* | Mass-media replication as a formal device; the triptych as pop-cultural reliquary. |
| 1960s–1970s | Francis Bacon's crucifixion paintings | The religious armature as vessel for extremity of feeling. [[entities/evil-biscuit|Biscuit]] cites Bacon for the Crucifella. |
| 1960s | Eduardo Paolozzi's early British pop | Comic strips, advertisements, machine parts collaged into postwar anxiety. Bauman names Paolozzi as an ancestor. |
| 1980s–1990s | Japanese neochibi / street-style tribes (Y2K Japan underground) | The visual base [[entities/charlotte-fang|Charlotte Fang]] transposed into Milady Maker: big eyes, subcultural fashion layering, cuteness as attitude. |
| 2010s | [[entities/parker-ito|Parker Ito]]'s early internet paintings | Painting that grew up terminally online. Monk Antony traces a straight line from Ito's 2010 work to traitmaxxing. |

## Contemporaries and forks (2021–present)

| Year | Node | Transfer |
|------|------|----------|
| 2021 (Aug) | Milady Maker (Charlotte Fang / Remilia Corporation) | The neochibi aesthetic, hypercitation, post-authorship, trait-based identity, drip-score logic. The intellectual and technical platform from which schizocollage forked. |
| 2022 (May) | Milady Cancel controversy | Charlotte Fang identified with "Miya" / suicide-cult-adjacent blog posts; forced to step down as Remilia CEO. This event fractures the Milady scene. Some participants later form the Solana avant fork. |
| 2022–2023 | Spiky DJ; [[entities/mifella|Mifella]] on Ethereum | A Remilia-adjacent avant character. Monk Antony: Mifella "struggling to mint out on Solana as the first of its kind on this new chain." The community jokes that Mifella would only mint out once the artist died. This joke becomes lore: Mifella sacrifices himself to birth the scene, resulting in Crucifella. |
| 2022–2023 | The Mifella schism (Ethereum → Solana) | A faction splits from Milady/Remilia and migrates to Solana. The schism point is the Mifella collection by Spiky DJ. From this fork: cuckcore, [[entities/vvv-so|VVV.so]], [[entities/yeche-lange|Yeche Lange]] gallery, and eventually the schizocollage idiom. |
| 2023 (Mar) | [[entities/evil-biscuit|Evil Biscuit]], *Drifella* | The first major schizocollage work: a punk mash-up of Milady, Mifella, and Dratini. Announced on X March 2023. |
| 2023 | Evil Biscuit, *The Constant Fella* | The Crucifella appears here; considered by some as Mifella 1.5. Lowbie called it a direct bridge to Drifella III's large-scale painterly turn. |
| 2023 | Ann Hirsch + Maya Man, *Ugly Bitches* | GAN-processed dolls paired with algorithmically drawn Instagram comments. Droitcour positions this adjacent to schizocollage as the AI-synthesis pole. |
| 2024 | Evil Biscuit, *Drifella 2* | The turn toward distortion. Earlier Drifellas were clearer; Drifella 2 is more mature, less legible. |
| 2024 (Apr–May) | Parker Ito / CreamyDreamy enters Solana NFTs | *[[entities/drilady|Driladys]]* — a play on @dril and "ladies/fellas" — collected on Solana. Ito's long institutional career gives the scene a bridge to the gallery world. |
| 2024 | [[entities/supermetal-bosch|Supermetal Bosch]], *Little Swag World* | AI-smoothed fashion figurines derived from mood-board collages. Exhibited at Yeche Lange in dialogue with Evil Biscuit. |
| 2025 | Evil Biscuit, *Drifella III* | 1,333 rooms × 3,999 paintings; the canonical exemplar of schizocollage in its most developed form. |
| 2025 (Feb) | Brian Droitcour, *Seams and Synthesis* (Le Random) | The first critical essay naming schizocollage as a formal category and opposing it to AI synthesis. |
| 2025 (2026-01 published) | Monk Antony (Peter Bauman), *Drifella III: Room for Complexity* (Le Random) | The pre-eminent close-reading of the central schizocollage work. |

## Open genealogical questions

- **The Hamilton-Warhol-Bacon-Rothko-Paolozzi-Cézanne line.** Bauman names them explicitly. No source shows the practitioners themselves citing these names. The lineage is critic-imposed scaffolding. Legitimate to note as such.
- **The Hamburg school.** Monk Antony suggested the Hamburg school "has a lot in common with this schizocollage/avant gay scene" in a reply thread. The connection remains anecdotal; no practitioner has claimed it.
