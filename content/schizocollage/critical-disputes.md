# Critical Disputes

## 1. The Charlotte Fang / Milady Cancel controversies and the schizocollage scene's inheritance

**Claim:** The schizocollage scene descends from Milady/Remilia via the Mifella schism, and therefore carries forward Charlotte Fang's frameworks (network spirituality, post-authorship, hypercitation, neochibi, left-hand style) even as it operates on a different chain and under different social conditions.

**Evidence for:**
- SonofLasG's Paragraph essay (October 2025) traces the genealogy explicitly: "From Milady to Forks."
- Monk Antony's Le Random essay names Milady as "the spiritual successor to nearly all of this Solana avant work."
- The visual grammar of schizocollage is unintelligible without neochibi body plans, trait-based identity, and drip-score logic — all Fang/Remilia innovations.
- The post-authorship model (characters circulating between artists' hands, no single author owning the lineage) is a direct extension of Milady's networked spirituality.

**Evidence against / complicating:**
- The Mifella schism (2022–2023) was a deliberate fork. Some participants attempted to restore relations with Remilia after the Cancel; others did not. wiki.remilia.org notes that Spiky DJ and others "attempted to restore relations" post-Cancel, which suggests the fork was not clean.
- Parker Ito, the most institutionally credentialed figure in the scene, has no documented connection to Remilia. His entry into Solana NFTs in 2024 is described as observation-based, not lineage-based.greyarealabs.co describes Ito as arriving "after observing the scene."
- No published schizocollage criticism (Bauman, Droitcour, SonofLasG, rightclicksave.com) mentions the Cancel as a topic the current scene addresses. The question of whether the scene reworks, distances from, or simply inherits Fang's frameworks in silence is open.

**Confidence note:** Medium. The frameworks are present; the scene's stance on their originator is unspoken in the critical record so far. The press's archival stance records what is on the record, which is: the frameworks are inherited, the founder's controversies are inherited, and no public statement of distance or endorsement has been documented.

## 2. "Avant/Gay" / "Gay NFTs" — self-naming, critic's frame, or both?

**Claim:** The "Avant/Gay" or "Gay NFT" label describes the schizocollage scene accurately.

**Evidence for:**
- Monk Antony's thread (2025) uses "Avant/Gay" as a genre designation alongside "Gay NFTs."
- The Verse Works podcast "ONE HOUR WITH LOWBIE" discusses "Avant Gay" and scene culture.
- Cuckcore.de's aesthetic vocabulary (pride flags, gay.gif, drag-adjacent works like *Drilady*) suggests partial embrace from inside.
- SonofLasG's essay title is "Iridium Pilled: Notes on the Avant Gay" and positions it as a named movement.

**Evidence against / complicating:**
- No single manifesto or artist statement has been found where a practitioner self-identifies primarily as an "Avant/Gay" artist rather than as a painter, collagist, or scene participant.
- The label appears more often in critical and curatorial speech (Bauman threads, podcast titles, SonofLasG's essay) than in practitioner self-description.
- The scene's actual composition includes straight-identified figures and figures whose gender/sexual identity is not public. The naming may be more about tone and camp than about demographic identity.

**Confidence note:** Medium. The label is in circulation and partially embraced, but its authority is curatorial rather than practitioner-declared.

## 3. The pop-art lineage: inheritance or critic-imposed scaffolding?

**Claim:** The schizocollage scene inherits a specific lineage from Hamilton, Warhol, Bacon, Rothko, Paolozzi, and Cézanne.

**Evidence for:**
- Bauman invokes all six names explicitly in his essay.
- Evil Biscuit cites Bacon directly in the Crucifella references and told Bauman about painterly composition.
- The triptych format, the room-scale installation, and the high/low equivalence are all moves those ancestors made.

**Evidence against / complicating:**
- No source has been found in which the practitioners themselves cite Hamilton, Warhol, Rothko, or Cézanne as direct influences. The Bacon reference is explicit (the Crucifella); the rest appear in critic-side scaffolding.
- Jared Madere's remark that Drifella 2 is "the most interesting conversation in painting in 30 years" is an artist's endorsement of the painting quality, not a lineage claim.
- Monk Antony's traitmaxxing lineage tweet traces photocollage and early photography, not the fine-art canon.

**Confidence note:** High for the Bacon reference (directly compositional); low for the rest as documentable inheritance. The wiki reports both positions.

## 4. Traitmaxxing: formal ambition or inflationary drift?

**Claim:** Trait-density is a formal virtue central to schizocollage.

**Evidence for:**
- Drifella III's 5,000+ traits and CryptoPunks comparison in Bauman.
- Traitmaxxing is treated as an honorific in the scene's discourse.
- The trait-density makes the works resistant to feed-scrolling and demands sustained attention.

**Evidence against / complicating:**
- Droitcour's essay positions trait-dissolution as the scene's next phase: "Traits don't disappear. They melt." Bosch's Little Swag World and Hirsch/Man's projects are not trait-based.
- The trait matrix is an infrastructural feature of platforms like OpenSea and Magic Eden. Overloading traits could be read as a critique of that infrastructure rather than an endorsement of it.
- Some collectors and critics may experience traitmaxxing as noise rather than richness.

**Confidence note:** High that trait-density is intentional; medium that its value is universally shared. The dispute is internal to the scene's self-understanding.
