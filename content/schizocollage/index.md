Schizocollage is the formal language of a contemporary on-chain art scene — the work appearing on Solana through [VVV.so](vvv-so), gathered at [Galerie Yeche Lange](yeche-lange) in New York, named and named again by Monk Antony in *Le Random*. Its central exemplar is [Evil Biscuit](evil-biscuit)'s *Drifella III* — 1,333 rendered rooms, each a triptych, totaling 3,999 paintings, built by misusing the HashLips PFP engine until the Dratini silhouette inside dissolves. The scene takes [Charlotte Fang](charlotte-fang)'s neochibi and post-authorship from Milady, splits eastward in 2022 through Spiky DJ's [Mifella](mifella), and develops on Solana into something Milady never attempted — image as built room rather than feed-scroll asset, lore as architecture, traits multiplied past the threshold where the figure remains legible.

The press's first issue exists because the scene's record lives in fragments. Two essays in *Le Random* by Peter Bauman and Brian Droitcour, one piece in *Right Click Save*, SonofLasG's *Iridium Pilled* on Paragraph, an hour of the *Verse Works* podcast with Lowbie, the cuckcore.de splash, and X.com threads scattered across @MonkAntony, @briandroitcour, @CreamyDreamy, @bis__cut, @MMifella, @Yeche_Lange, @vvvdotso, @archivepilled, @sssluke1. Nothing collects them. Nothing treats the scene as a coherent formal development on its own terms — only as Vibes. The press treats it as a movement deserving the same vocabulary one would bring to any post-war school, and assembles the record in one place so a reader in five years has somewhere to begin.

What is collected here: the figures producing the work, the works themselves, the venues where the work appears and is sold, the concepts the scene has named for itself, and the argument the press is making — lineage, formal grammar, adjacent aesthetics, critical disputes. The accompanying [Are.na channel](https://www.are.na/clio-press/schizocollage) holds the visual evidence: more than a hundred image blocks of Drifellas, Mifellas, Driladys, Mons, *Little Swag World* pieces, install shots from Yeche Lange's *Fella Royale*, and the secondary press the wiki cites by name.
