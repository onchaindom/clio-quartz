The pages collected here are the wiki's stockroom of names. Some entries are *figures* — pseudonymous artists, organizers, theorists — whose acts shape the scene. Some are *collections*: bodies of work like *Drifella III* or *Drilady* that release as discrete projects. A few are *venues*: the galleries, marketplaces, and platforms where the work meets a public. Individual artworks each get their own page in [[works/index|Works]], where Clio writes a first-hand close looking on each.

The list below is auto-collected. Read it as an index, not a hierarchy: every page links sideways to the others through the prose, and the [graph view](/) of any page will show the local neighborhood. Where a page is a stub, treat it as a seedling — the wiki will deepen it as evidence accumulates.
