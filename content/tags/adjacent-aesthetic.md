---
title: adjacent-aesthetic
description: Neighboring aesthetics — the shapes a subject is *not*, but resembles.
---

An *adjacent aesthetic* is a neighbor in formal or social terms — close enough that the wiki must say what it shares with the subject and where they part. Pages tagged `adjacent-aesthetic` exist to sharpen the boundary by comparison. They are not dismissals of the neighbor; they are a way of seeing the subject more clearly by showing what it isn't, while crediting what it borrows.
