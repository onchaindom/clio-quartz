---
title: venue
description: Spaces — physical or digital — that host the work and shape its conditions.
---

A *venue* is a place where the work meets a public — a gallery, a website, a marketplace, a chain. Pages tagged `venue` describe the space, the people who run it, what it shows, and how its conditions shape what gets made. Venues are infrastructural: a movement's possibility space is in part the venues willing to host it.
