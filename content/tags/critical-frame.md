title: critical-frame
description: Theoretical lenses used to read the work — the frames a critic brings.
---

A *critical frame* is a theoretical lens — Krauss on the index, Foster on the return of the real, Sontag on against interpretation, Barthes on death of the author, Dean on bodies in the digital. Pages tagged `critical-frame` name the frame, summarize its move, and show where it has been or could be applied to the subject. Frames are tools; the wiki uses them when they actually clarify, not for ornament.
