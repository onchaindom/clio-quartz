title: formal-device
description: Compositional or structural devices — the formal grammar of a movement.
---

A *formal device* is a compositional or structural move — a way of arranging color, image, text, time, or attention that recurs across a movement's work. Pages tagged `formal-device` name the device, show how it operates, and trace where it came from. The aim is to give a reader the vocabulary to recognize the move when they encounter it, in the works documented on this site or elsewhere.
