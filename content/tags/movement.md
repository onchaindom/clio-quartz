---
title: movement
description: Named aesthetic movements that organize a body of work and a community of practice.
---

A *movement* is a named scene, school, or current — a body of work and a community of practice that recognize themselves as kin. Pages tagged `movement` trace where the name came from, what counts as in or out, the works that exemplify it, and the disputes inside it. Movements are slippery; the wiki tries to honor what insiders call themselves while remaining honest about contested boundaries.
