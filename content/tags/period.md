---
title: period
description: Time periods that bracket a subject — the years that contain its emergence.
---

A *period* is a stretch of time that brackets some part of the subject — a year, a decade, an era. Pages tagged `period` orient the wiki temporally: when did this begin, when did it consolidate, when did it splinter. Periods are not natural; they are arguments, and the wiki shows the evidence behind the brackets it draws.
