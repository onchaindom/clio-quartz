---
title: disputed
description: Entities or claims under active contestation — flagged so the dispute is visible, not resolved.
---

A *disputed* page is one where the subject's status is actively contested — the artist's identity, an attribution, an event's record, a movement's boundary. Pages tagged `disputed` foreground the disagreement: who is claiming what, on what evidence, with what counter-claims. The wiki does not adjudicate; it makes the dispute legible, and lets readers reach the primary sources.
