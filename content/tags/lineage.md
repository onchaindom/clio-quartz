---
title: lineage
description: Historical filiation — the chains of cause, descent, and inheritance behind a subject.
---

*Lineage* pages map descent: who studied with whom, what work begat what, which earlier movement seeded the next. Pages tagged `lineage` are explicit about evidence — when was the influence acknowledged, by whom, in what venue. The Clio Press model treats lineage as load-bearing; a movement that doesn't know where it comes from cannot be honestly read.
