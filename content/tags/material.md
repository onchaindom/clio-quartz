---
title: material
description: Physical or digital substrates — the stuff the work is made of.
---

A *material* is what a work is made of — paint, vinyl, code, JPEGs, the blockchain itself, paper, sound. Pages tagged `material` describe the substrate and what it does for the work: what it permits, what it forbids, what it costs, what it carries forward. Material is not background; the wiki treats medium specificity as evidence about meaning.
