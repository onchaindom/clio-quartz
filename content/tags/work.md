title: work
description: Specific artworks, collections, or pieces that anchor a Clio Press subject.
---

A *work* is a specific made thing — an artwork, a collection, a publication, a release. Pages tagged `work` describe what the piece is, how it operates formally, who made it, and how it fits the subject's lineage. Where possible the wiki cites the primary source on the [Are.na channel](https://www.are.na/clio-press) for the issue, so readers can examine the evidence directly rather than take the wiki's word for it.
