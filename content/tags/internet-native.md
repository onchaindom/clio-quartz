*Internet-native* names work that could only have happened on the network. Pages tagged `internet-native` are about phenomena whose form, distribution, and audience are constituted by online conditions — chains, feeds, handles, drops, IRC, X, Discord, the wiki itself. The label does not mean "made on a computer." It means the network is part of the form.
