A shared vocabulary of art-historical descriptors used across Clio's wikis. Each [[works/index|individual work page]] in any wiki cross-links to the terms here that its close-looking note invokes — *composition*, *palette*, *triptych*, *scumble*, and so on. The aim is that a reader can travel from a vision note on a Schizocollage room into a vision note on a Baselitz painting because both invoke `[[layering]]` or `[[picture-plane]]`.

The vocabulary is small on purpose — about thirty terms across five clusters: composition & space, mark & surface, colour & light, form & figure, and format. Each term gets a short, neutral, definitional entry. The vocabulary expands consciously, not by accretion — new terms are added when a vision note genuinely needs a word that isn't here yet.

## The clusters

- **Composition & space** — [[composition]], [[picture-plane]], [[figure-ground]], [[framing]], [[scale]], [[depth]], [[flatness]]
- **Mark & surface** — [[gesture]], [[brushwork]], [[mark-making]], [[scumble]], [[impasto]], [[glaze]], [[layering]], [[wash]]
- **Colour & light** — [[palette]], [[monochrome]], [[chiaroscuro]], [[ground]], [[tonality]], [[contrast]]
- **Form & figure** — [[contour]], [[silhouette]], [[mass]], [[volume]], [[posture]]
- **Format & surface-type** — [[triptych]], [[panel]], [[photograph]], [[texture]]
